# Lorenzo Portillo Cogollo

**Oceanographic Engineer | Research Scientist | Environmental Data Science**

I am an Oceanographic Engineer and Research Scientist at the Marine and Coastal Research Institute (INVEMAR), Colombia. My work combines physical oceanography, satellite remote sensing, environmental time-series analysis and reproducible scientific computing.

## Research interests

- Marine and coastal environmental variability
- Satellite ocean colour, chlorophyll-a, and sea-surface temperature
- Climate variability and extreme events
- Environmental data harmonization and quality control
- Hydrodynamic modelling and Earth observation
- Interdisciplinary applications of environmental indicators

## Tools and methods

- **Programming:** Python, MATLAB
- **Scientific data:** NetCDF, satellite products, oceanographic observations, environmental time series
- **Methods:** Quality control, temporal and spatial harmonization, statistical analysis, geospatial visualization
- **Currently learning:** R for reproducible environmental and geospatial analysis
