Lorenzo Portillo Cogollo
Oceanographic Engineer | Research Scientist at INVEMAR | Environmental Data Science

I am an Oceanographic Engineer and Research Scientist at the Marine and Coastal Research Institute (INVEMAR), Colombia. My work integrates physical oceanography, phytoplankton ecology, satellite remote sensing, environmental time-series analysis, and numerical modelling to better understand marine and coastal variability.
Research interests
- Physical oceanography and coastal climate variability
- Satellite ocean colour, chlorophyll-a, and sea-surface temperature
- Marine phytoplankton ecology and taxonomy
- Climate variability, extreme events, and ocean–atmosphere interactions
- Environmental data harmonization, quality control, and geospatial analysis
- Hydrodynamic modelling and reproducible scientific computing
- Cross-disciplinary applications of environmental information to decision-making
Tools and methods
- Programming: Python, MATLAB
- Scientific data: NetCDF, Sentinel-2 imagery, satellite ocean-colour products, oceanographic observations, and environmental time series
- Methods: Data quality control, spatial and temporal harmonization, statistical analysis, geospatial visualization, and numerical modelling
- Currently learning: R for reproducible environmental and geospatial analysis
